# Batch allocation

Allocate over the complete requested topic or production batch. Use deterministic largest-remainder targets and a rolling ledger; do not force every small review shard to mirror the final percentages.

## Default quotas

| Dimension | Target |
|---|---|
| Carrier | T-shirt 20%, long-sleeve 20%, sweatshirt 20%, tote 20%, all others 20% |
| Camera distance | close 20%, medium 50%, environmental 30% |
| Single-model gender | male 30%, female 70% |
| Apparel side | front 80%, back 20% |

Carrier suitability and an artwork's explicit front/back design override the numerical target for that item. Record the override so the final report distinguishes semantic exceptions from allocation drift.

“Other” carriers include mug, cushion, blanket, fridge magnet and later supported products. Allocate this 20% by topic fit and existing inventory. Small carriers keep realistic physical scale while receiving a clean, visible placement in the 3:4 frame.

Count gender only for visible single-model images. Couple and family roles are reported separately so a multi-person scene does not distort the single-model quota. Treat gender presentation as a batch casting dimension and vary faces, hair, age impression and styling within it.

Back views need a lived action such as walking, turning, dressing, cycling or looking outward. Keep the print clear of hair, straps and outerwear. Environmental views must preserve the scene while leaving the carrier and print legible; move toward a medium-environmental frame when a true wide shot hides the product.

Also track style card, scene family, indoor/outdoor, actor structure, action family, lighting family and dominant palette. Avoid three consecutive candidates sharing the same style card, imaging tendency, scene, camera distance or main action.

Before generation, report current count, target count and next deficit for each hard quota. At completion, report requested versus actual counts and every documented override.

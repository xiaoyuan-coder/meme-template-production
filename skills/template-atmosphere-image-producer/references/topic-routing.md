# Topic and subject routing

Classify the template from its artwork, title, description, tags, prompt and runtime semantics before casting the scene. A topic profile may refine these defaults.

| Route | Default scene subjects | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| `pet_relationship` | person + matching pet + product | Everyday companionship or a shared activity; the pet has an independent intention |
| `pet_product` | matching pet + product; person optional | Resting, sniffing, following, playing or using the product in a plausible way |
| `standalone_print` | model or product; no semantic pet | Styling, street, home, still life or editorial product use driven by the artwork |
| `couple` | two partners + product | A shared task, outing, private joke, gift or anniversary moment with distinct actions |
| `family` | family group + product | Home, celebration, travel or routine activity with credible age and relationship cues |
| `fandom_music` | model or friend group + product | Concert preparation, rehearsal, listening, collecting or everyday support behavior |
| `character_anime` | model or product + fitting environment | Youth lifestyle, collection space, street or interior scene without unrelated animals |
| `occasion` | relationship structure implied by the event | Gift, gathering, holiday or commemoration with restrained seasonal cues |
| `custom` | explicitly planned | Record why a standard route does not fit |

Actor count follows the relationship encoded by the template. Use a product-only scene when a person adds no useful wearing, handling, force or relationship information. Mugs, cushions, blankets and magnets often work without people; apparel usually benefits from a wearer.

Every visible subject receives an intention, attention target and plausible next movement. Couple and family scenes use shared causality or parallel participation. Avoid synchronizing every face, gaze and pose around the print.

Extract one echo from the template's action, prop, place, costume, color or cultural setting. Translate it into the scene only when it remains natural. The photograph's credibility and atmosphere remain the higher gate.

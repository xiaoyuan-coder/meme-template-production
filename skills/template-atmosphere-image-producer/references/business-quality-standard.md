# Business quality standard

## Product promise

The atmosphere image is the template gallery's primary merchandising photograph. It should make a viewer want the customized product while showing a believable result. Judge it in this order:

1. Would a person stop because the photograph is attractive?
2. Does the scene feel lived-in, physically coherent and human?
3. Can the viewer understand the carrier and printed artwork?
4. Does the scene express the template's topic and relationship?
5. Is there one memorable but unforced visual idea?

A technically correct mockup can remain below the pass line when it lacks atmosphere. An attractive photograph fails when the print, subject identity or product physics is wrong.

## Hard failures

- A face, body, hand, pet or contact point reads as synthetic or anatomically implausible.
- The image has muddy yellow-brown or gray-green color, compressed midtones, plastic skin or uniform AI smoothing.
- The subject performs a catalog pose, forced smile, print-pointing gesture or rigid staged interaction.
- The template becomes a sticker, badge, framed rectangle or floating layer on the product.
- The print is oversized, covers implausible folds, retains an unwanted exterior background, or conflicts with carrier color.
- Template text, species, subject count, dominant color, marking or defining relationship changes.
- The carrier or artwork cannot be recognized at the intended gallery thumbnail size.
- The chosen actor structure conflicts with the topic, such as adding a pet to an unrelated standalone print or using one model for a couple design.
- The output is not exactly 3:4.

## Pass and Good Case

`approved` means the photograph, product, print and topic all work without a material defect. `good_case` is an additional flag on an approved final and requires a strong first impression, clear photographic language, a lived relationship or action, natural print integration, and a memorable composition or echo.

Keep Good Case learning dimensional. Record what succeeded: photographic atmosphere, human truth, color, action, composition, carrier physics, print treatment or semantic echo. A Good Case does not prove every unmentioned dimension.
